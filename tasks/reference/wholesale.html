A FEW THINGS TO KNOW.

BEFORE YOU
GO BIG.

Can I mix products in one enquiry?

Yes. Add different hookahs and accessories to your list and set a quantity for each. Availability and any order requirements are confirmed when we discuss your quote.

What are the minimum quantities and rates?

Minimum quantities and wholesale rates are confirmed per quotation. The list collects your requirements; it does not promise a price or reserve inventory.

Can I request a preferred delivery date?

Include your preferred date and delivery city in the enquiry. Delivery availability, charges and timelines are confirmed before an order is accepted.
